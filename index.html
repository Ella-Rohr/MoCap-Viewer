MoCap Viewer Help:

Upload a .glb file by selecting Upload File and using the play and pause buttons.

How to pan the viewer
  1. Hold down on the left mouse to rotate around the model.
  2. Hold down on the right mouse to pan around the model.
  3. Pinch or scroll to zoom in and out.
  4. Use the Gizmo in the bottom right to rotate around the model.

To close select the ? again.
